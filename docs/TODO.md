## Close the C2 gap

**The one rule this session paid for four times: fix at the USE site, never at the capture site.** A change where a primitive is *consumed* — the wrap `JOP_GENOP` performs on its own operands, the `JOP_VSTORE` and `JOP_STORE` arms, the argcast loop — is bound to the right node by the op's own recorded snapshot, and every such change landed. A change where a cast or a lowering *happens* — a mark at `gen_cast`, a region bracketing `gen_negf` — perturbs the very stream `rir_verify` replays, and every such change failed: twice by dropping `faithful`, once by aborting the compiler. Read the banked negatives below before designing another attempt.

Instruments, in the order they pay: `RIRDUMP=1` for ops either side of the blamed index with byte windows; `-DRIR_DBG_OPTRACE=1` plus `RIRDBG=<funcname>` for the `[ent]` entry stream, `[stmt]` naming which entry pushed each BasicBlock statement, `[vst]` printing the call-tail spill test's inputs, `[gop]` printing the operand wrap's admission inputs, and diffable `PARSE`/`C2` op streams; `MCC_REPLAY_IR=6` for `[rir-dump]`/`[rir-diff]` node pairs. A structural `[rir-dump]` diff against the tree found most of what closed this session, each time from one body.

### What closed, and the shape that worked

- The **code-free cast on a 32-bit target** was the biggest class and is now down to `overflow_narrow.c::main`. `(int)` over a 64-bit value, or over an `unsigned int` `size_t`, changes neither size nor register, journals no `JOP_CVT_*`, and leaves the arena node wrong. All of it closed by widening the wrap `JOP_GENOP` already performs on its own operands: `rir_eff_size` and `rir_eff_unsigned` walk down through untyped Binary/Unary to the first typed node, and a genuinely **wider** grandchild, or an **unsigned** one where the operand's recorded type is signed and the same size, overrides the admission tests. Narrowing direction only. That closed `signbit_inline.c::main`, `popcount_inline.c::ref_clrsb64`, `div_mod_shift.c::main` and `cmp_invert.c::main`.
- The same class **at a call argument** closed `bitfields.c::main`: the argcast wrap skipped nodes that are already an `AST_Convert`, and now lets one through when that Convert is genuinely wider than the argument's recorded type.
- riscv64's **`gen_negf` spill** closed 3 of 4 bodies. `save_reg(gv(bt))` spills the double before the in-memory sign flip; `JOP_STORE` declined it because its guard wants an lvalue where this is a register-resident rvalue. The rvalue arm stores the VALUE and hands back **the slot as an lvalue** — handing back a `Load` over it dereferences twice and every body dies with "pointer expected". Restricted to float spills.

### Banked negatives — do not re-pay for these

- **Marking code-free `gen_cast` calls**, three variants: at every such call wrapping the shadow-stack top → `c2ok 531/1146`, `c2invalid=517`, two core dumps; narrowed to a const-only `AST_Binary` outside every region → `1110/1145`, `c2err=3`, `faithful` down one; bound to the operand the marker's own vstack snapshot names → `faithful` holds but `c2err` 0 to 1-3 on every key, x86_64 1130 to 1126, and neither target body moves. Stamping the bound node's type instead of wrapping is worse again (x86_64 1115, `c2err=5`).
- **Bracketing `gen_negf` as `RIR_R_FNEG`** on the `RIR_R_INC` pattern **aborts the compiler**: `ok=` falls by 2 to 4 on riscv64, arm64 and arm, `faithful` 1111 to 1076 and 1171 to 1134. Region markers are part of the captured stream.
- **Relaxing the `!lv` guard in `JOP_STORE` generally**: -30 `c2ok` on every key, `c2err` 2 to 7. Integer register spills are everywhere and each becomes a BasicBlock statement. The float restriction is what makes it safe.
- **Dropping the argcast wrap's `AST_Convert` skip** without the wider test **segfaults** every PE key — `ok=` falls by 1 to 2 on five keys. The crash was a null `type.ref` reaching `type_size` for an enum, latent in two existing helpers; they now take the operand's real ref.
- **Widening `rir_child_width_differs` itself** to the effective width: same `c2ok` as the landed shape, minus 14 to 20 `arenahasheq` on **every** key. Keep it on immediate children and OR the narrowing test in beside it.
